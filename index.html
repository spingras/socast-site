socast privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-19

What socast does

socast captures the audio that other apps on your phone play and streams it over your local Wi-Fi network to the Sonos speaker you choose.

Data we collect

None. socast has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app sends nothing to the developer. The only internet connection it makes is to Google Play, to check whether you bought the unlock.

Purchases

The unlimited-casting unlock is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy; socast never sees your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the unlock. As with any Play purchase, Google gives the developer an order record (such as order number, date, country and price) for accounting and refunds.

Audio

Audio is processed in real time on your phone and sent only to a speaker on your own network. It is never stored or shared.

Permissions

  • Microphone: Android requires this permission for capturing app audio. socast does not record from the microphone.
  • Screen and audio capture prompt: Android asks for your approval each time casting starts.
  • Local network and Wi-Fi state: used to find your speakers and send them play, stop and volume commands.
  • Notifications: an ongoing notification is shown while casting, with a Stop button.

Children

socast is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Trademarks

socast is an independent app and is not affiliated with Sonos, Inc. Sonos is a trademark of Sonos, Inc.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
